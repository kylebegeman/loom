# L39 references

Every dimension the geometry uses, with its source. Values are nominal; tolerances are noted
where the source gives them.

## Credit card (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1)

- 85.60 × 53.98 mm, thickness 0.76 mm (±0.08), corner radius 2.88 to 3.48 mm. The geometry uses
  3.18 mm, the midpoint.
- Source: [ISO/IEC 7810, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO/IEC_7810). The standard
  itself is paywalled; Wikipedia quotes it.

## US quarter

- Diameter 24.26 mm, thickness 1.75 mm.
- Source: [United States Mint, Quarter](https://www.usmint.gov/learn/coins-and-medals/circulating-coins/quarter).

## 1 euro coin

- Diameter 23.25 mm, thickness 2.33 mm.
- Sources: [European Central Bank, euro coins](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/pdf/material/A4_Gate_text_EN_web.pdf),
  [1 euro coin, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_euro_coin).

## AA battery (IEC LR6)

- Maximum length 50.5 mm, maximum diameter 14.5 mm, positive terminal maximum diameter 5.5 mm
  with minimum height 1.0 mm. The geometry uses the maxima: body 49.5 mm plus a 1.0 mm button.
- Sources: [Farnell LR6 datasheet](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/3999358.pdf),
  [AA battery, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AA_battery).

## LEGO 2×4 brick

- 31.8 × 15.8 × 9.6 mm body (height without studs), studs on an 8.0 mm pitch, stud diameter
  4.8 mm.
- Stud height: sources give 1.6 to 1.8 mm. The geometry uses 1.7 mm. **Not verified to one
  value.**
- Sources: [Zoe Blade, Lego brick dimensions](https://notebook.zoeblade.com/Lego_brick_dimensions.html),
  [Bill Ward, Brick Geometry (BrickCon 2018)](https://www.brickpile.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/brick-geometry-bbtb2018.pdf).
- LEGO is a trademark of the LEGO Group. The label names the brick descriptively; the shape is a
  plain box with cylinders, not a copy of any LEGO design file.

## M3×10 socket head cap screw (ISO 4762)

- Head diameter 5.5 mm, head height 3.0 mm, nominal thread diameter 3.0 mm, length 10 mm
  measured under the head (so 13 mm overall). The 2.5 mm hex socket and the thread are omitted.
- Source: [Engineering Hardware, M3 socket head](https://engineeringhardware.com/guide/size/m3-cad-svg-014-sockethead/).

## Hand

- Length 190 mm, wrist crease to middle fingertip. This is an adult average, not a standard.
- ANSUR II (US Army, 2012) hand length means, from the public data files as summarised by
  secondary sources: about 193 mm for men, 181 mm for women, 189.3 mm combined. Hand breadth
  (across the knuckles, excluding the thumb) averages about 85 mm combined, 88 mm for men. The
  outline uses a palm about 85 mm wide.
- Sources: [ANSUR II data, Penn State OpenLab](https://www.openlab.psu.edu/datasets/ansur2/),
  [Sota2 ANSUR II hand summary](https://www.sota2.com/research/sota/hand-anthropometry-on-ansur-ii-combined-male-and-female),
  [MATEC hand anthropometry study](https://www.matec-conferences.org/articles/matecconf/pdf/2017/33/matecconf_imeti2017_01044.pdf),
  [sizefit, average hand size](https://sizefit.org/guides/average-hand-size).
- **Partly verified:** the split male and female figures come from secondary analyses of the
  public data, not the official ANSUR II report (Gordon et al., TR 15/007). ANSUR measures from
  the wrist bone (stylion), slightly longer than from the crease. 190 mm is a fair round adult
  size either way.

## Prior art

- Physical practice: makers photograph prints next to a coin, ruler or battery.
- Online 3D viewers (for example Sketchfab's scene setups and some slicers' "human scale"
  figures) show scale figures beside models. Not verified feature by feature; mentioned only as
  the common pattern.
