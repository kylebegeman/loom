## Manual check

With Kyle's permission, in a dev client against a worktree `.t3`, on web and desktop:

1. Open a clean bracket STL: "Ready to print", eight green rows, under a second.
2. Open a part with a thin fin (or set the nozzle to 1.0 mm in settings): the walls rows fail,
   pins appear on the fin, clicking a row frames it, Escape clears the selection.
3. Open a SCAD part, drag a Customize slider: the Part section marks the report stale, reruns
   once after the drag settles, and the badge follows.
4. Toggle "Print check pins" in the display menu and "Hide pins" in Markup; capture a view with
   pins shown and confirm they are in the image.
5. Open a GLB from `loom_pcb_export_reference` (`.loom/pcb-references/`): the check runs and
   reports shells for the board and parts.
6. Open a large STL (2,000,000 triangles or more): the panel stays responsive while it checks;
   "Cancel" stops it.
7. Ask an agent to check the open part with `loom_model_preview_3d_check`, then to fix the worst
   finding and check again. Turn off "Let agents render models and propose variants" and confirm
   the tool is refused.
8. With L35: lay the part flat on another face; first layer and overhangs change and pins stay
   on the surface.
9. From a remote browser over the tailnet: same report, nothing large downloaded beyond the
   viewer's mesh.
10. Light and dark themes, and the narrow inspector sheet.

Upstream server: the 3D panel is unavailable, nothing to check. Single-file executable build:
the section shows "Print checks are not available in this build."
